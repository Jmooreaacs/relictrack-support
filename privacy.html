RelicTrack Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

In short: RelicTrack has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not run servers that collect your data, and we do not sell or share your personal information. Your hunts, targets, trails and photos stay on your device.

Who we are

RelicTrack (“the app”) is a field guidance app for metal detecting and relic hunting. Questions about this policy can be sent to jmooreaacs@aol.com.

Information the app uses, and why

  • Location (GPS). Used while the app is open to guide you to targets, show your position on the map, record your breadcrumb trail, measure distance from base camp, and add coordinates to photos. The app only asks for location access while you are using it.
  • Camera and photos. Used only when you take a field photo of a find or site. Photos are saved inside the app on your device with their location. They leave your device only if you choose to export or share them.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Used for voice commands such as “target” or “next.” Speech recognition is provided by Apple. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process audio on its servers under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive or store your audio.
  • Bluetooth. Used to connect to the optional RelicTrack wrist wearable, and to play the app’s spoken guidance and alerts through, and take voice commands from, Bluetooth earbuds or a headset. We do not collect or store any information about the Bluetooth devices you connect.
  • Data you create. Hunt names, your name, targets, notes, zones, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Online services the app contacts

To find historic sites, maps, and weather, the app sends requests to the services below. These requests can include the coordinates of your base camp or current position, or the text of a search you ask for. Each service has its own privacy policy, and we do not control how they handle requests.

  • Map and imagery providers (for example Esri/ArcGIS, USGS National Map elevation, and OpenStreetMap-based services including Overpass and Nominatim)
  • Wikipedia, Wikidata, DBpedia and similar reference databases
  • U.S. National Weather Service (weather.gov)
  • U.S. Bureau of Land Management and state historic-property data services
  • DuckDuckGo, for web searches you request

Most of the app’s historic-site data is bundled inside the app and works without a connection.

Purchases and free trial

RelicTrack includes a 7-day free trial, after which a one-time in-app purchase unlocks the app. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. To tell whether the trial has started or the app is unlocked, the app keeps a trial start date and your purchase status in your device’s secure Keychain. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or contact details.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data lives on your device. You can delete individual hunts, targets and photos inside the app. Deleting the app removes its stored data from your device.

Children

RelicTrack is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Responsible use and the law

You are responsible for following all applicable federal, state, tribal and local laws and regulations when you use RelicTrack. This includes laws about metal detecting, relic and artifact collecting, archaeological and historic resources, cemeteries and burial sites, and access to public, tribal and private land. Always obtain permission from the landowner or managing agency before searching, and respect protected sites and posted rules. Target locations and land-agency information shown in the app are for general guidance only and may be incomplete or out of date. They are not legal advice and do not give you permission to search or dig anywhere. Do not use the app while driving.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
