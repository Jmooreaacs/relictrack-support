Common questions

The app asks for permissions. Why?

Location guides you to targets and records your trail, the camera takes field photos, the microphone and speech recognition power voice commands, and Bluetooth connects the optional wrist wearable and your Bluetooth earbuds or headset. You can change any of these in Settings > RelicTrack on your device.

Voice commands are not responding.

Make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed for RelicTrack in Settings, that the armed circle on the main screen is green, and that your device is not muted by another app or call. If you use Bluetooth earbuds, try reconnecting them.

How do I move a target or base camp?

Press and hold the target or base camp on the map for about one second until it shakes, then drag it to the new spot and release.

Do I need an internet connection?

Most historic-site data is built into the app. Live maps, weather and web searches need a connection. You can download map areas for offline use from the Tools menu before you head out.

How does the free trial and purchase work?

RelicTrack is free to download and includes a 7-day free trial. After the trial, a one-time purchase unlocks the app permanently. There is no subscription. If you already bought it, or you reinstall or switch devices, tap Restore Purchase on the unlock screen, using the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my data?

Delete hunts, targets and photos inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

My GPS position looks off.

GPS accuracy depends on sky view and your device. Allow Precise Location in Settings, wait a few moments outdoors for a good fix, and use the calibration option in the app if your position is consistently offset.

Responsible use

Please follow all applicable federal, state, tribal and local laws when you hunt, and get permission from the landowner or managing agency before you search. Land-agency and site information in the app is for general guidance only and may be incomplete. See the Privacy Policy for more.
